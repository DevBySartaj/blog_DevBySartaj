Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

At DevBySartaj, accessible from sartajpage.site, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by DevBySartaj and how we use it.

When you interact with our website, such as when you fill out our contact or collaboration form on the Work With Us page, we may collect personal information from you, including:

  • Full Name

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and services

3. Log Files

DevBySartaj follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, DevBySartaj uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

DevBySartaj's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party services for more detailed information.
